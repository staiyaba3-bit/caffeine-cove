    ABOUT CAFFEINE COVE · EST. 2025 YOUR LITTLE ESCAPE

    Not Just Coffee. A Feeling.

    Founded as an antidote to the rushed world, Caffeine Cove is a neighborhood sanctuary where artisanal coffee, warm companionship, and mindful slow living come together.

    🌿 100% Single-Origin Arabica
    🥐 Fresh Daily Hearth Bakery
    ☕ Small-Batch Local Roast
    🕯️ Warm Acoustic Ambience
    A Corner For Every Mood

    A Sanctuary Designed for Slowing Down

    Every detail of Caffeine Cove was crafted with intention. From our bentwood chairs and hand-turned ceramic mugs to the amber pendant lighting and gentle lo-fi melodies, we created a third space where time feels unhurried.

    Whether you are journaling in a sunlit corner, catching up with an old friend over a Velvet Latte, or seeking a quiet nook to read, our doors are open with open arms and freshly brewed coffee.

    THE FOUR PILLARS OF OUR CRAFT
    01

    Ethical Bean Sourcing

    Direct-trade relationships with organic smallholder farms in Chikmagalur, Huila Colombia, and Ethiopia. Fair living wages and sustainable shade-grown harvests.

    02

    Small-Batch Profiling

    Roasted locally twice every week in 12kg cast-iron drums. We meticulously profile every origin to celebrate floral aromatics, natural caramelization, and berry sweetness.

    03

    9-Bar Precision Pour

    Every morning, our baristas dial in the espresso dose to within 0.1g accuracy, matched with custom mineral-remineralized brewing water for silky microfoam.

    04

    Hearth-Baked Daily

    Our bakery fires at 6:00 AM daily with French butter croissants, rich Belgian brownies, and artisanal Basque cheesecake made without artificial preservatives.
